- Added (#1423): `oss-workspace` opens with about two seconds of terminal fireworks -- two rockets,
  a burst, a shimmer, a cycle of spinner verbs, then the tagline -- before it does anything else.
  Only when stdout is a terminal and `TERM` is not `dumb`; `OSS_WORKSPACE_NO_FIREWORKS` set to
  anything skips it; a strict POSIX `sleep` that refuses fractional seconds runs the frames back to
  back rather than failing the launch. Nothing after it depends on it.
