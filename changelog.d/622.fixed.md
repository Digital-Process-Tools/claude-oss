- `/oss:scaffold` and `/oss:doctor` now print the whole corrected `.supertool.json`
  when a repo's radar board is registered but not routed (`no-route`) or has no tiers
  registered at all (`no-tiers`), instead of only a fragment a maintainer has to merge
  by hand into a config with two independently silent halves (`presets` and
  `ops.radar.radar_tiers`). Getting one of the two right by hand produced a board that
  is byte-identical from outside to a healthy one -- exactly the state the check
  exists to make visible. `.supertool.json` itself is still never overwritten; only
  what is printed changed. The remaining state, `route-unknown` (an unreadable
  `presets`), still prints only the fragment, deliberately: a merge cannot safely
  decide what to append an unreadable value to (#622).
