- Composing a lane is one call, not four (#1143). `lane_setup.py --claim` claims the issues, registers
  the lane, validates the brief and renders the whole `Agent(...)` call, with the fleet label's
  multiplier derived from the issues that actually came back `claimed` rather than from a list retyped
  into a second call. It refuses to render on a structural brief finding, on a `subagent_type` outside
  `KNOWN_AGENT_TYPES`, and when the primary issue is not held. `lane_setup_label.py` is gone, folded
  into `lane_setup.py`; `brief_schema.py` is now `lane_setup_brief_schema.py`, a module rather than a
  command, following the same "no longer a standalone CLI" shape #1069 gave the selection submodules.
