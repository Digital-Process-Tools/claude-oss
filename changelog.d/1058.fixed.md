- Reject a `{m,n}` interval in `user_visible_paths` whose magnitude exceeds 255, the lower
  of the known grep-family limits (BSD grep's measured `RE_DUP_MAX`; GNU grep documents a
  higher one) -- `oss_config._brace_interval_problem` previously validated only the
  arrangement of an interval, not its magnitude, so a value like `a{99999}` was accepted
  and then silently and permanently disabled the generated changelog gate at release time
  (`grep -Eq` exits 2, SYNTAX ERROR, which the guard cannot tell apart from a genuine
  no-match) (#1058).
