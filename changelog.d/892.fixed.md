- `doctor_check_worktree_reap_permission.py`: a bare-wildcard **deny** rule
  (`Bash(git *)`) for `git worktree remove` or `git branch -D` rendered
  `absent` -- "nobody granted this" -- rather than reporting that the op may
  already be deliberately forbidden. `_bash_wildcard_allow_detail` (#886) was
  allow-side only; its new deny-side sibling, `_bash_wildcard_deny_detail`,
  now catches this shape. Reported as its own state, `cannot-tell-whether-
  forbidden`, kept separate from #886's `cannot-tell-whether-covered` so the
  direction of the ambiguity -- might be covered vs. might be forbidden -- is
  never lost (#892).
