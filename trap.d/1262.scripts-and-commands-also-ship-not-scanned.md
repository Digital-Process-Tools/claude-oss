While fixing #1262 (widening tests/test_content_invariants.py's home-path leak
guard from trap.d/ alone to SHIPPED_MID_LANE_DIRS = trap.d/, changelog.d/,
docs/, tests/, bin/, hooks/, schemas/), a self-review audit (oss:auditor
spawn) found that scripts/ and commands/ are tracked, not gitignored, and
ship in the installed plugin artifact exactly the same way as the six
directories that were added -- confirmed with
`git ls-files | awk -F/ '{print $1}' | sort -u`, which lists both alongside
the six.

They are edited by a lane under the same "mid-lane, by an agent" conditions
as tests/ -- the concrete leak #1262 fixed (a real absolute home path
hardcoded in a tests/*.py fixture) is exactly the class of leak that could
land in scripts/*.py or commands/*.md too, not only in free-text logs.

Checked at the time: a grep for a home-path shape across scripts/ and
commands/ found one hit (scripts/transcript_refusals.py, using this repo's
own established single-letter placeholder-username convention for fixtures),
so there is no live leak today -- but the guard's own scope simply does not
reach either directory, and its own comment claims to enumerate "every
shipped, mid-lane-written directory," which is not true while these two are
absent.

Not fixed as part of #1262: scripts/ (86 files) is a much larger surface
than the six directories in scope, and extending the code-directory
allowlist mechanism (HOME_PATH_PLACEHOLDER_USERNAMES) to it would need its
own false-positive sweep the same way tests/ did -- a real but separate
piece of work, out of the blast radius of a fix scoped to six named
directories. Left here rather than filed as an issue: not blocking a
release (ranking table: misreports, non-blocking, can ship behind a
trap.d fragment).
