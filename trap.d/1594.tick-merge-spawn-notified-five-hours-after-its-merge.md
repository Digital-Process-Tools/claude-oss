# A tick-merge spawn notified five hours after its merge landed

A tick-merge spawn notified completion 5.3 hours after the merge it owned had landed, and its
own final message said the notification was stale: it had superseded its background CI poll by
re-checking directly (`gh-branch:624fdb1` returned GREEN) and had already reported.

Observed 2026-09-16 in a `/oss:run` scheduler session. The spawn merged PR #1594 at about 11:50
CEST; its task notification reached the scheduler at 17:05 CEST, reporting `completed` with a
duration of 18,970,014 ms. Four ticks and a release (v0.37.1) ran in between.

Two costs, neither large on its own.

The spawn stayed resident for hours after its work was done, holding a background poll nothing
was waiting on. The poll had already been made redundant by the spawn's own direct re-check,
which is the correct move; what did not happen is the poll being cancelled once it was redundant.

The notification arrives with the same shape as a live completion. A scheduler reading it at
17:05 has no way, from the notification alone, to tell a spawn that just finished real work from
one whose work landed before the previous release. Only the spawn's own first sentence
disambiguated it, and that sentence exists because the agent happened to say so rather than
because anything required it. A scheduler that acted on it -- resuming it, or treating its
subject PR as newly merged -- would have been acting on a five-hour-old fact.

Worth checking whether a spawn that has sent its report can close its own background work rather
than leaving it to expire, and whether a task notification can carry the age of the result it
announces.
