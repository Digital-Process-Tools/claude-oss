`commands/doctor.md` names `/oss:tick` as the next step on a report whose own WARNs say to run
`/oss:scaffold`, and nothing checks the condition it states.

Raised by the maintainer reading a live report on 2026-09-09, not by a check. The section reads:

    Name **`/oss:tick`** as the next step once the report above is clean, or once every `FAIL` and
    every actionable `WARN` is resolved.

The condition is correct and there is nothing that evaluates it. `commands/doctor.md` has no branch
for "some actionable WARNs are still open, name their remedy instead", so a reader who skims the
verdict lands on `/oss:tick` while the report immediately above says, in two separate lines:

    WARN trap.d/README.md: not in this repo. Run /oss:scaffold.
    WARN .oss/statusline.py: re-running /oss:scaffold would change what it does -- _win_candidate_names,
    _safe_which, _doctor_field, _default_branch_marker, and 14 more.

The sharp part is that the information needed to get this right has already been computed.
`report_with_remedy()` attaches a paste-ready remedy to every WARN it builds, and #1065 deliberately
put per-finding remedies in `doctor.py` while leaving the overall next step to the command surface --
a good split, for the stated reason that `doctor.py` also runs mid-tick and before a release and has
no standing to say what happens afterwards. What the split did not carry across is the derivation:
the command surface names one fixed next step instead of reading the remedies the findings already
produced.

`VERDICT: usable with gaps` reduced to pass/not-pass is where it goes wrong. The report distinguishes
an actionable WARN from an informational one, and the next-step section does not. Measured on this
run: 8 WARNs, and exactly 2 point at scaffold. The rest are explicitly not actionable in that sense --
`stale merged branches` is marked report-only, `channel consumer pin` says cosmetic and calls a
deliberate pin legitimate, `vanished worktrees` says the mechanism is not identified in this plugin's
own code. So a strict reading of "resolve every actionable WARN first" is wrong in the other
direction too: it would stall the loop forever on a cosmetic pin nobody intends to clear.

The fix is a rule in `commands/doctor.md`, not in `doctor.py`, and it keeps #1065's split intact:
derive the next step from the findings' own remedies -- if any WARN carries a `Run /oss:scaffold.`
remedy, name that first and say which lines it clears; name `/oss:tick` when none does. That also
gives the reader the actionable/informational split the report already knows and currently discards.

Ranked `misreports`: a surface names a next step its own report contradicts. Non-blocking, so this is
a fragment rather than an issue, per the routing rule in `skills/manager/phases/findings.md`.
