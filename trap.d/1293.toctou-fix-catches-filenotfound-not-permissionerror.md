delete-pending or a sharing violation) rather than `FileNotFoundError` -- the exact
"platform-specific exception types, so a narrow `except` never fires" shape agents/developer.md
names for the platform band. trap.d/1228.site3-race-recurred-on-release-commit-dd2353e.md
records the original failure on the `windows-latest, 3.12` CI leg, so the race the fix closes
is demonstrably reachable on that platform -- just not necessarily through the exception type
the fix now catches.

Fix shape: `except (FileNotFoundError, PermissionError)`, or an `OSError` arm that reports
rather than silently continuing.
