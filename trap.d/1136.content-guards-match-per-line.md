Editing prose in `commands/*.md`, `agents/*.md` or `skills/manager/**.md` and a content guard
goes red on a phrase you did not touch: `tests/test_command_references.py` matches **per line**,
not across the whole document.

Observed 2026-09-06 during #1136's deletion pass over `commands/setup.md`. Nothing about the
sentence changed -- no word added, removed or reordered -- but excising a paragraph above it
reflowed the text, and the phrase `only line that asks the forge` ended up split across a newline.
`_separates_the_offline_plan_from_the_forge_read` failed. The fix was to re-wrap so the phrase sits
on one line again; no wording changed at all.

Cost: one confusing red leg in a pass where several other guards were failing for real reasons, so
the first reading was "my cut removed a rule" when the cut had removed nothing.

Worth knowing because it inverts the usual advice. Normally a content guard failing means you
deleted something load-bearing and should put it back. This one fails on **layout**, and the
correct fix is to re-wrap rather than to restore. Check whether the asserted phrase spans a line
break before concluding you cut too much.

Confirmed by re-wrapping the single line and re-running:
`python3 -m pytest tests/test_command_references.py -q --no-cov` went from `1 failed, 404 passed`
to `405 passed`.

Adjacent, same pass: `--no-cov` is worth adding when running a small subset, because
`pyproject.toml`'s `addopts` carries `--cov-fail-under=85` and prints
`FAIL Required test coverage of 85% not reached. Total coverage: 3.62%` after a perfectly green
3-file run. That line names no test and no file, and reads as a failure when nothing failed.

**Second instance, same pass, different file, different agent, found independently.**
`skills/manager/SKILL.md`: a rewrap split the literal `deferring to the next tick is not a decision`
across a newline and `_says_deferring_is_a_stall` failed. Again nothing about the sentence changed.

Two independent hits in one pass over different documents makes this a property of the guards
rather than an accident: **any deletion pass that reflows a paragraph can break a content guard
without changing a word.** Expect it, and check the line break first.
