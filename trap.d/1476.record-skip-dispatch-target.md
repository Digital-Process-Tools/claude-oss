Issue #1476's own body names a second observation, separate from the branch-reading bug the
issue's fix closes: `next_action.record_skip(state_path, candidates, taken_source, reason, at=None)`
(and its CLI wrapper `_record_skip_cli`) refuses `taken_source` unless it is one of `rank()`'s own
ranked sources (`inbound`, `release`, `curate`, `triage`). `dispatch` -- what a tick falls through to
when nothing is due -- is never a ranked source, so a deliberate fall-through to dispatch over a
due-but-skipped candidate cannot be recorded through this mechanism at all. The only way to record
that decision today is a free-text `oss_state.py --decision` call, invisible to
`next_action.py`'s own repeat-suppression receipt (`_route_already_seen`) -- a curate/triage backlog
skipped in favour of dispatch six ticks running would look, from this receipt's point of view,
identical to one that was never skipped at all.

This is not a bug in the current diff: `record_skip`'s refusal is deliberate and well-argued in its
own docstring (a self-review finding from #1405's own follow-up: a typo or hallucinated source name
must not sail through and land in the state file's permanent decision log as confidently as a real
deviation). The gap is that `dispatch` has no ranked-source identity to be validated against in the
first place, since it is not a `rank()` candidate -- it is what happens when there are no candidates
(or none are taken).

Confirmed by reading `scripts/next_action.py`'s `record_skip()` (the `known_sources` check) and
`rank()`'s own `DEFAULT_ORDER` (`inbound`, `release`, `curate`, `triage` -- `dispatch` never
appears). Not reproduced against a live tick, since this trap was found by reading the code while
implementing #1476 rather than by hitting the refusal directly.

Possible shapes for `/oss:curate` to weigh, not a decision: (a) treat `dispatch` as a distinguished,
always-valid `taken_source` for `record_skip`, since it is a real, name-able outcome even though it
is not a ranked candidate; (b) leave `record_skip` as-is and give the free-text
`oss_state.py --decision` path its own dedicated, named shape (e.g. a `--took-dispatch` flag) so the
decision at least gets a consistent field to search for later, even if it stays outside the
repeat-suppression receipt; (c) decide the gap is acceptable since dispatch is the do-nothing-special
case and a skipped backlog will keep re-ranking as due anyway on the next tick, so nothing is
actually lost except a receipt of *why* it was skipped.
