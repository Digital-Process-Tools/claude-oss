findings.md` (~line 170, #1705) tells a filer to run `gh-labels:repo:OWNER/NAME`
to check a label spelling on another repo. The installed supertool refuses it:
"gh-labels takes at most one argument, got 2 ... Syntax:
gh-labels[:tally=PREFIX]". The same paragraph says "nothing matches: omit the
label" -- a filer who reads that refusal as "nothing matched" silently drops
`filed-by-loop`, which is the exact #1705 defect this paragraph exists to
close. What would settle it: name a call that actually works (e.g. `gh label
