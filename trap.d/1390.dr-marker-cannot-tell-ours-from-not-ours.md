The first end-to-end /oss:run (2026-09-11, second launch of the 0.32.0 launcher)
diagnosed, repaired nothing, and left the statusline reading `dr◐`. The maintainer
read that as the diagnose step not having done its job. Doctor's own output at the
same moment:

    WARN auto-update: could not check (8 minute(s) ago) -- the install record could not be read
    WARN channel census vs channel:health: could not compare -- cached reading older than its interval
    WARN channel MCP connection: every MCP server resolving to the consumer reports a failed transport
    WARN statusline channel: the cached channel:health reading is older than its own refresh interval
    VERDICT: usable with gaps -- 4 warning(s), 4 notice(s)

None of the four is the loop's to repair. The install record was mid-rewrite during
the launcher's own 0.31.0 -> 0.32.0 update (file mtime 15:34, the check ran in that
second; `plugin_update.py --print-state` reads `current 0.32.0` a minute later). The
other three are one session's channel consumer state and two cache clocks -- a bound
session clears them, nothing in a diagnostic can.

So `/oss:run` step 1 was right to repair nothing, and `dr◐` was right to render, and
the maintainer was right to be unsure -- because the marker carries one bit
(a WARN survives) where the question has two: does a WARN survive, and is it one the
loop could clear. #1064 already named the failure mode -- "a single WARN nothing can
clear pins this marker permanently" -- as a defect in the check that raised it. This
instance is the other reading: the check is fine, the WARN is real, and it is
simply not ours. Rendered identically.

Cost: one exchange, and the maintainer's confidence in the first autonomous run,
which is not nothing on the day it first ran.

Possibly the fix is in doctor rather than in the statusline: each WARN already knows
whether its remedy is a manual op, a scaffold run, or a session-level fact (the
`doctor-warning-lifecycle` vocabulary entry says every WARN is clearable by one of
the first two, and these four are none of them, which makes them a third kind that
entry does not name). Give that third kind its own level -- or its own mark in `dr`
-- and `◐` means "yours to do" again.
