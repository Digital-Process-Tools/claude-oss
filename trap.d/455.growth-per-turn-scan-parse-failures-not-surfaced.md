Gate 3 round-1 audit for the v0.28.0..HEAD delta found scripts/growth_per_turn_scan.py's
`analyze_growth` (lines ~214-220, ~247-257) counts `non_blank_lines` and `parsed_records`
internally, but the all-lines-failed branch is the only consumer of that pair. A transcript
where most lines fail `json.loads` still returns `ok: True` with a `growth_per_turn` computed
from whatever did parse, and neither `non_blank_lines` nor a skipped-line count appears in the
returned dict or in `run()`'s report. The module docstring's own three-state contract
(`measured` / `no-post-fix-transcripts` / `could-not-read`) is honoured only at whole-file
granularity, not per-transcript.

Fix shape: carry `non_blank_lines - parsed_records` into the `ok: True` result and sum it per
side, the same way `turns_with_unusable_usage` already is.
