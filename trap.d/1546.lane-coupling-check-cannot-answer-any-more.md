Found by the v0.35.0 gate 3 release audit, round 1, ranked `fails-to-preserve`.

`labels.lane_patterns` was deleted from `.oss.json` (#1530) and its validation removed from
`oss_config.validate()`, but `scripts/doctor_check_lane_coupling.py:89-97` is still wired into
`doctor.py` and still reads that key. It now returns `not-configured` permanently for this
repository, so #1244's whole-repo test-coupling guard can never fire here again -- and the ~40
`labels.lane_coupling_allowlist` entries still in `.oss.json` are inert config keying nothing.

Not silent, which is why it is not ranked higher: the NOTICE text says exactly why it cannot check,
which is the honest third state.

What makes it a finding is the inconsistency **inside one delta**: `doctor.py:3017` removed
`check_vanished_worktrees` outright in this same range with the stated reason "Removed rather than
left reporting a permanent `unknown`, which would be a check that can never answer" -- and the
identical situation two checks away was left in place doing precisely that.

What would settle it: whether `labels.lane_patterns` is meant to come back, or the check is meant
to follow the registry out.
