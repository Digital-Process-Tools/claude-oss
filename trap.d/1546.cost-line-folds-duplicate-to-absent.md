Found by the v0.35.0 gate 3 release audit, round 1, ranked `misreports`.

`scripts/tick_handback.py:236-245`: `_find_optional_field` returns `None` when its pattern matches
zero times **or** more than once, so a handback carrying two `COST:` lines is recorded identically
to one carrying none. `cost` is the measurement #1499 exists to obtain, so an agent that reported
its own spend twice is read by accounting as one that never reported at all.

This is deliberate and argued in the module docstring, with `release_handback.py`'s `GATE:` as
precedent, so it is a claim about a trade rather than an oversight. What would settle whether the
trade is still right: whether accounting ever distinguishes `cost: None` from "cost was not asked
for". While it does not, the two are the same value and the duplicate case costs nothing; the
moment it does, a duplicated line becomes a silent zero in the only number the loop is trying to
drive down.
